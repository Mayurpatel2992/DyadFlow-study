Rating 3D talking heads

Each page shows one short conversation (16 s) between two people. You hear both voices. The grey 3D head belongs to one of the two people: sometimes it speaks, sometimes it listens to the other person. A badge on each video shows SPEAKING or LISTENING as it plays.

Every page has four computer-generated versions of the same head in the same conversation (Video A–D, in random order). Watch each one with sound and rate it from 1 (very poor) to 5 (excellent), separately for the two phases:

While speaking
Lip sync: do the lips and jaw match the speech?
Naturalness: does it move like a real person talking (smooth, no jitter, not frozen or robotic)?
While listening
Reactions: does it respond to the other person in a believable way (nods, expressions, attention)?
Naturalness: does it move like a real person listening (smooth, no jitter, not frozen or robotic)?

10 pages, about 12 minutes. Headphones help. Your answers are saved in this browser, so you can close the page and continue later.

“Play all together” plays the four videos in sync with one sound track. You can also play each video on its own.
